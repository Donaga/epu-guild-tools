# EPU Guild Tools

Guild bank, crafting and guild management for **E Pluribus Unum** on WoW: Forever, with a two-way link to the guild Discord.

**Guild bank**
- Needs list with progress bars, a shopping list, and bank contents across bank characters and the guild bank
- Requests: ask for an item, officers approve and mail it (the mail is filled in for you)
- Donations logged automatically; a Donate panel at the mailbox and gold coins on needed items in your bags

**Crafting**
- Every guild crafter's recipes, searchable, with materials, skill-up colors and designated guild crafters
- Craft requests sent straight to a crafter; tooltips show who in the guild can make an item

**Guild**
- Roster with level, zone, roles and a "near my level" filter
- News: announcements (also from Discord) and milestones like level-ups and first mounts
- Officer tools: member review, private officer notes, an officer log

Type `/epu` in game to open it. This page is the recommended way to install it: click **Install** and the CurseForge app keeps it updated. Data syncs between guild members who have the addon; nothing is sent outside the game except through the guild's own Discord bot.

Source and issues: https://github.com/Donaga/epu-guild-tools
