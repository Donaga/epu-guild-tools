# Bank gear for you

Open it with `/epu bankgear`, the **Gear for you** button on the main window's **Bank** page, or the **Bank gear for you** tile in **Member tools**.

It lists weapons and armor that the guild bank or a bank alt holds and that the character you're playing can use.

## What counts as fitting you

- **Level:** the item's required level is yours, or up to five levels ahead. Those show "Wear it at level N".
- **Armor type:** the type your class wears at the level you'd wear the item. Warriors and paladins wear mail until 40 and plate from 40. Hunters and shamans wear leather until 40 and mail from 40. Rogues and druids wear leather, and priests, mages and warlocks wear cloth. Cloaks, rings, necklaces, trinkets and off-hand items fit everyone.
- **Shields and relics:** shields for warriors, paladins and shamans. Librams for paladins, idols for druids, totems for shamans.
- **Weapons:** the types your class can train. You may still need to visit a weapon master before you can use one.

The rules are the class rules of the original game. Class-only items (a set piece, say) aren't checked, so read the tooltip.

## Upgrades

Each item is compared with what you wear in that slot by item level. Rings and trinkets compare with the weaker of your two. A one-hander compares with your weaker hand for classes that dual wield, and with the main hand for the rest. An off-hand item next to a two-hander compares with the two-hander. An empty slot counts as an upgrade.

Upgrades come first, then what you can wear now, then the rest by item level. **Upgrades only** hides everything that isn't an upgrade. Gear more than ten levels below you only shows when it's an upgrade.

## Getting it

- **Pull:** at the open guild bank, takes it straight into your bags, from tabs your rank may withdraw from and within today's withdrawal limit.
- **Request:** files a bank request for one, noted "Bank gear", that the bank managers see and send.
- **Requested:** you already have an open request for it.

Item info can take a moment to load the first time. The window counts what's still loading and fills in as it arrives.
