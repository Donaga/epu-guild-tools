# Guild bank by category

A window that shows the whole guild bank grouped by kind of item (Cloth, Herbs, Consumables, Weapons & Armor and so on), like Baganator's category view of your bags. Baganator's guild bank window only shows tabs, so this one is EPU's. Nothing is ever moved for you.

Open it with **Guild bank view** on the main window's **Bank** page, or `/epu vault`.

- One button per item, with the total across every tab. Hover it for how many each tab holds, and whether it's out of place in its tab (an orange dot marks those, same as `/epu banktabs tidy`).
- **By category** or **By tab**, a tab picker, and a search box. Click a section header to fold it.
- Away from the bank it shows the guild bank as it was last seen.

## At the guild bank

- **Right-click** an item: take a stack into your bags, from a tab where you still have withdrawals today.
- **Drag** an item: pick it up. **Shift-click**: link it in chat.
- **Drop** an item from your bags on the window: it goes into the tab its kind lives in, onto a stack of the same item when there's room, otherwise an empty slot.

## With the bank: Off / Beside / Instead

The menu at the top right of the window, or `/epu vault beside`, `/epu vault instead`, `/epu vault off`.

- **Off** (default): it opens only when you open it.
- **Beside**: it opens next to the guild bank window every time.
- **Instead**: it opens in place of the guild bank window. The game's guild bank window, and Baganator's if you use it, move off the screen until the bank closes. **Other window** brings them back for that visit, for gold and the bank log. Closing this window closes the bank.
